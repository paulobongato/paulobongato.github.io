The Mysteries of the Rosary

Put each set of Mysteries in the order they happened

The Rosary has four sets of Mysteries, and each set has 5 events from the life of Jesus and Mary. In this quiz, the 5 events in a set will be shuffled — your job is to put them back in the order they really happened!

  • There are 4 rounds: Joyful, Luminous, Sorrowful, and Glorious Mysteries (20 events total).
  • Drag each card into place, or use the ▲ ▼ buttons, so the events go from first (top) to last (bottom).
  • Press Check My Order when you're ready. You'll see what you got right and learn the correct order.
  • At the end, you'll get your total score and a review of anything you missed.
